# The kitchen-diner's whole-room floor — does Azerbaijan publish one?

Findings for **ticket 70, *A zone floor is posted on the whole room***.

`docs/research/az-statutory-floor-transcription.md` §10 item 3 left this
open, in these words:

> *"The correct whole-room statutory floor for a `mətbəx-yemək otağı`. The norm
> does not publish one. This note establishes that 6.0 is not it; it does not
> establish what is."*

This note is the search for what is. **It did not find one, and the negative is
now established rather than assumed.**

**C8 applies to every number below.** These are dimensional standards with a
citation, not a compliance claim. §8 states the legal force of each source in
the profile's own `source_force_vocabulary`. Nothing here is *claimed* to a
Homeowner and no surface text names a law.

**This note edits no artefact.** Ticket 70 owns the decision; `experiments/warp/`
is claimed by four other tickets and is not written here.

## 0. TL;DR

**No instrument with legal or normative force in Azerbaijan publishes a
whole-room area for a `mətbəx-yemək otağı`. The 6,0 m² the engine posts is the
only figure the law attaches to that room, and it is attached to a zone inside
it.** Ticket 70's premise survives contact with the sources.

## 0.1 The one-line answer to the question as asked

> **Does any instrument with legal or normative force in Azerbaijan publish a
> whole-room area figure for a `mətbəx-yemək otağı`?**

**No.** Exactly two Azerbaijani instruments name the room type — AzDTN 2.7-2 and
2.7-3 — and both give it a **zone** figure and no room figure. Its statutory
ancestor did not know the room type at all. **All nine other norms in the 2.7
family were obtained and swept; the term occurs zero times in any of them**, and
the two that could plausibly have carried a dwelling schedule defer back to
2.7-2 instead. No amendment, no state housing agency and no Azerbaijani teaching
source supplies the number.

The gap is **deliberate and structural, not an omission from a single clause**,
and two independent lines of evidence say so. §3: the norm's own definitions are
built on the zone distinction and are internally consistent with leaving the
room open. §10.2: **the entire regional family drafts this way** — Russia
zone-only, Kazakhstan two zones and still no room, Belarus no room areas at all.

⚠️ **Confidence in the negative is high, and its limits are stated.** The
searching that could still overturn it is listed in §11 — and none of the eight
items there could produce a figure with *live Azerbaijani force*, which is what
the question asked for.

## 2. The negative, established exhaustively

### 2.1 The method

Rather than re-reading one clause, the full extracted text of both dwelling
norms was swept for every occurrence of the room type and of every
kitchen-related term. The counts are the finding.

### 2.2 AzDTN 2.7-2 — every occurrence of the room type

`mətbəx-yemək otağı` occurs **twice**:

1. **The definitions clause**, defining the type and attaching **no number**:

   > «mətbəx - yemək otağı- **mənzildə yeməyin hazırlanması və qəbulu üçün ayrıca
   > zonaları olan otaq**;»

   *"kitchen-dining room — a room in the apartment **having separate zones for
   the preparation and consumption of food**."*

2. **cl. 5.7**, attaching a number to the **zone**:

   > «- mətbəx-yemək otağında **mətbəx zonası** - 6 m²-dən.»

   *"— in the kitchen-dining room, the **kitchen zone** — from 6 m²."*

The complete list of kitchen-related m² figures in the whole of AzDTN 2.7-2 is
three, all in cl. 5.7:

| clause text | translation | on what |
|---|---|---|
| «mətbəx - 8 m²-dən» | kitchen — from 8 m² | the **room** |
| «mətbəx-yemək otağında mətbəx zonası - 6 m²-dən» | in the kitchen-dining room, the kitchen zone — from 6 m² | a **zone** |
| «sahəsi 5 m²-dən az olmayan taxça-mətbəx» | a kitchen-niche of not less than 5 m² | the **niche** |

There is no fourth. **No whole-room figure for the kitchen-diner exists in this
norm.**

### 2.3 There is no dining zone anywhere

`zona` occurs in AzDTN 2.7-2 in a room-area sense **once** — the `mətbəx zonası`
of cl. 5.7. Every other occurrence is a smoke-control *air zone* in the fire
sections. `yemək zonası` **does not occur**. The same holds in AzDTN 2.7-3.

**This forecloses the tidiest version of ticket 70's option 2.** "Kitchen zone
6 m² plus the dining zone the norm requires" cannot be computed, because AzDTN
requires no dining zone area. Any dining allowance must come from the ergonomic
layer, and is therefore ours.

### 2.4 AzDTN 2.7-3 — the same shape, in a recommending register

`mətbəx-yemək otağı` occurs **twice**: in cl. 4.7, which merely admits the type —

> «Fərdi yaşayış evlərində yaşayış otaqları və yardımçı sahələr - mətbəx (**o
> cümlədən mətbəx-yemək otağı**), dəhliz (holl), sanitar qovşağı nəzərdə
> tutulmalıdır.»

*"In individual dwelling houses, living rooms and auxiliary spaces — kitchen
(**including the kitchen-dining room**), corridor (hall), sanitary unit — shall
be provided."*

— and in cl. 5.1, which gives the same zone figure:

> «- mətbəx - 9 m2;
> - mətbəx-yemək otağında mətbəx zonası - 6 m2;»

Note the register. cl. 5.1's areas are introduced *«…aşağıdakılardan az olmamaqla
qəbul edilməsi **tövsiyə edilir**»* — *"it is **recommended** that they be
adopted as not less than the following"*. **The whole of AzDTN 2.7-3's area
schedule is recommendatory**, which is why the profile already degrades it to
`market_default` / `recommended`.

## 3. Why the gap is structural — the norm's own taxonomy

This is the analytically load-bearing part of the note, and it is the piece the
transcription note did not have.

### 3.1 A plain `mətbəx` already includes eating

AzDTN 2.7-2's definitions clause distinguishes three kitchen forms, and the
distinguishing feature is **where food is eaten**:

> «mətbəx - **kulinariya və qida qəbulu üçün** nəzərdə tutulmuş otaq;»
>
> *"kitchen — a room intended for **cookery and the taking of food**."*

> «taxça-mətbəx - elektrik pilətəsi və mexaniki sorucu-vurucu ventilyasiya ilə
> təchiz edilmiş, yemək hazırlamaq üçün nəzərdə tutulmuş, **lakin yemək qəbulu
> ərazisi olmayan** sahə;»
>
> *"kitchen-niche — a space equipped with an electric hob and mechanical supply-
> and-extract ventilation, intended for preparing food **but having no
> food-consumption area**."*

> «mətbəx - yemək otağı- mənzildə yeməyin hazırlanması və qəbulu üçün **ayrıca
> zonaları** olan otaq;»
>
> *"kitchen-dining room — a room having **separate zones** for the preparation
> and consumption of food."*

The ladder is explicit and it is about eating, not about size:

| type | eating | floor |
|---|---|---|
| `taxça-mətbəx` | **no** eating area | 5 m² |
| `mətbəx` | eating, **not zoned** | 8 m² |
| `mətbəx-yemək otağı` | eating, in its **own zone** | **kitchen zone** 6 m² |

### 3.2 So 6 m² is a relaxation of the cooking part, and the room is the *larger* form

Read down that ladder, the 6 m² stops being puzzling. A `mətbəx` must hold both
functions in one undifferentiated room, so it is floored at 8 m². A
`mətbəx-yemək otağı` has pulled eating out into a zone of its own — so the norm
lets the **cooking zone** fall to 6 m², *because it no longer has to
accommodate the table*.

**The kitchen-diner is not a smaller room than a kitchen. It is a bigger room
with a smaller kitchen part.** Posting 6,0 on the whole Room therefore does not
merely under-constrain by an unknown margin — it inverts the norm's own ordering,
floor­ing the zoned-and-therefore-larger room **2,0 m² below** its unzoned
equivalent.

⚠️ **Three routes to a whole-room number. Two agree at ≈8; the third says 12.**

| # | route | arithmetic | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Taxonomy.** The unzoned room that does the same two jobs floors at 8 m²; the zoned room does not do less. Corroborated in СП 54.13330, whose `кухня` is likewise *«с обеденной зоной»* at 8 m² — §10.1. | `mətbəx` floor | **8,0** |
| **B** | **Zone plus ergonomic allowance.** The statutory kitchen zone, plus the profile's own ergonomic `dining` `min_area`. | 6,0 + 1,9 | **7,9** |
| **C** | **The nearest norm that actually quantifies it.** СП РК 3.02-101-2012 cl. 4.4.4.21 floors the cooking zone at 6 m² **and the dining zone at 6 m²** — §10.1. | 6,0 + 6,0 | **12,0** |

A and B share no input — one is AzDTN's definitions, the other the
region-invariant ergonomic layer — and land 0,1 m² apart. **C is 50 % higher
than both, and the disagreement is real rather than a rounding artefact.**

⚠️ **The three are not measuring the same thing, and the note does not pretend
they converge.**

- **A is a lower bound** and a firm one: below 8,0 the engine floors the zoned
  room beneath its unzoned equivalent, which contradicts the norm's own
  ordering. It says *not less than*, not *this is the number*.
- **B is a minimum-viable dining allowance** — a 4-person table with body zones
  and nothing else. It is what the room needs to work, not what a regulator
  would sign off.
- **C is a regulator's actual choice** in the closest structural analogue, and
  it is a *comfort* judgement, not a clearance one: the Kazakh definition
  requires a dining zone sized for *«единовременного приема пищи всеми членами
  семьи»* — the whole family eating at once. ⚠️ It also sits on a more generous
  schedule throughout (kitchen 9–12 m² against AZ's 8), so it cannot be
  transplanted at face value.

**The honest reading: AzDTN's own materials support ≈8 m² as a floor, and the
distance from there to Kazakhstan's 12 is precisely the discretion AzDTN chose
to leave with the designer.** A number picked anywhere in 8–12 is defensible; no
number in that band is Azerbaijani law.

⚠️ **None of the three is a transcription and none may be labelled one.** AzDTN
publishes no whole-room figure; all three are **reasoning about** what it
publishes. If ticket 70 takes option 2, the value is `engine_choice`, its
`force` is **not** `statutory`, and this section is its derivation. ADR 0023's
refusal to quantify in the opposite direction is the standard it has to meet.

The family runs to **2.7-11**, and **every member was obtained and swept**.
The string `mətbəx-yemək` occurs **zero times** in all nine non-dwelling members.

**2.7-7 and 2.7-9 both defer rather than restate.** AzDTN 2.7-7 cl. 6.1.12:
*«Yaşayış və ictimai yerləşgələri birgə yerləşdirərkən, AzDTN 2.7-2-də müəyyən
edilmiş məhdudiyyətlər nəzərə alınmalıdır»* — *"when residential and public
premises are co-located, the restrictions established **in AzDTN 2.7-2** shall be
observed."* The two mixed-use norms that could most plausibly have restated a
dwelling schedule explicitly point back at 2.7-2 instead.

⚠️ **AzDTN 2.7-6 is the interesting near-miss, and it cuts the other way.** The
dormitory norm defines `taxça-mətbəx` as a space *«yemək hazırlamaq üçün nəzərdə
tutulmuş, **lakin yemək üçün sahənin olmadığı** köməkçi yerləşgə»* — for
preparing food **but having no space for eating**. Family units get a living
room (≥12 m²) **and a separate** `mətbəx` (≥5 m²) — two distinct rooms, never
merged. Shared kitchens are sized per-capita. Azerbaijani norm-drafting handles
the eating/cooking relationship by **zones and separate rooms**, never by a
combined-room area.

**2.7-1 is car parks, confirmed twice first-hand** — in the 2010-era register,
and in AzDTN 2.7-2's own normative-references list, which reads
*«12. AzDTN 2.7-1 — Avtomobil dayanacaqları»*.

**2.7-9 was the only real candidate and it was checked properly.** A brand-new
norm — Collegium decision 3-35/3-211/2025 of **18 September 2025**, in force
**30 September 2025**, State Register **15202509183211**, *«İlk dəfə qəbul
edilir»* — covering high-rise buildings, which in Azerbaijan are largely
residential. Its full 97-page text contains **no minimum room areas for
dwellings**, and no occurrence of `mətbəx-yemək otağı`. `mətbəx` appears only in
an apartment electrical-circuit list and in a live-loads table row for
*ictimai binaların mətbəxləri* — public-building kitchens. It is a fire,
structural and building-services norm that **defers dwelling planning to AzDTN
2.7-2**, which it cites in its normative references.

## 6. The statutory ancestor, and its Azerbaijani amendments — minima.md §11.4 closed

### 6.1 The repeal is explicit and it is in AzDTN 2.7-2's own front matter

> «Bu texniki normativ hüquqi akt qüvvəyə mindiyi tarixdən СНиП 2.08.01-89\*
> "Жилые здания" normativ sənədin Azərbaycan Respublikası ərazisində hüquqi
> qüvvəsi **dayandırılır**.»

*"From the date this technical normative legal act enters into force, the legal
force of the normative document SNiP 2.08.01-89\* 'Жилые здания' on the
territory of the Republic of Azerbaijan is **terminated**."*

Effective **30 November 2021**. **The ancestor and everything amending it are
dead law.** Whatever they contain cannot supply a live Azerbaijani figure — a
point worth holding onto before reading the rest of this section.

### 6.2 The amendments were found, and «кухня-столовая» is not in them

The three acts minima.md §11.4 recorded as *"could not be located"* are
published in Russian on the **`/ru/` locale mirror** of the same arxkom URL
whose Azerbaijani twin now serves AzDTN 2.7-2 — the Azerbaijani page was updated
at the 2021 supersession and the Russian one never was.

A 7-page package bundling **Изменение №1, №2, №5 and №6**, each stamped:

> «Azərbaycan Respublikası Dövlət Tikinti və Arxitektura Komitəsinin "28" 04
> 2004-ci il tarixli 04 №-li qərarı ilə təsdiq edilib və 01.07.2004-ci il
> tarixdən Azərbaycan Respublikası ərazisində qüvvəyə minib.»

*"Approved by decision No. 04 of 28 April 2004 of the State Committee for
Construction and Architecture of the Republic of Azerbaijan and in force on the
territory of the Republic of Azerbaijan from 1 July 2004."*

**The decisive count: «столов» occurs ZERO times in the package.** «кухня-столовая»
— the kitchen-dining room — **does not exist in Azerbaijani law before 2021.**
Every area figure is for a standalone `кухня`. There is no older, fuller rule
that AzDTN 2.7-2 cut down to a zone; the room type arrives in 2021 *already*
carrying only a zone figure.

### 6.3 And they settle §11.4's conjecture — half right

§11.4 guessed the AZ divergences were inherited from these acts. Now testable.
**Amendment №1, п.2.4**, verbatim:

> «Площадь гостиной (общей комнаты) в однокомнатной квартире не должна быть
> менее **14 м²**, в квартирах с числом комнат 2 и более – не менее **16 м²**,
> других жилых комнат и кухни – не менее **8 м²**. В однокомнатных квартирах
> типа 1А и двухкомнатных типа 2А городских домов допускается проектировать
> кухни или кухни-ниши не менее **5 м²**.»

**Amendment №1, п.1.1**: «Высота жилых помещений от пола до потолка должна быть
не менее **2,7 м**». **Amendment №2, п.7**: mansard bedroom and kitchen may be
**7 м²** provided the common room is ≥ 16 м².

| AzDTN 2.7-2 (2021) value | in the 2004 AZ amendment? | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| height **2700 mm** | **yes** — п.1.1, *«не менее 2,7 м»* | ✅ §11.4 **confirmed** — Azerbaijani since 2004 |
| kitchen **8 m²** | **yes** — п.2.4 | ✅ inherited |
| kitchen-niche **5 m²** | **yes** — п.2.4 | ✅ inherited |
| mansard **7 m²** | **yes** — Amdt №2 п.7 | ✅ inherited |
| living room 2+ otaq **16 m²** | **yes** — п.2.4 | ✅ inherited |
| living room 1 otaq **15 m²** | **no — the amendment says 14** | ❌ §11.4 **refuted** — new in 2021 |
| two-person bedroom **10 m²** | **no — no such rule exists** | ❌ §11.4 **refuted** — new in 2021 |
| kitchen zone **6 m²** | **no — the room type is absent** | ❌ new in 2021 |

**minima.md §11.4's hypothesis splits.** The vertical datum is a genuine
Azerbaijani inheritance from 2004. The two provisions that arrive in 2021 — the
10 m² two-person bedroom and the 6 m² kitchen zone — did **not** come up the
Azerbaijani SNiP line.

⭐ **But "invention" is the wrong word for them, and §10.1 says where they came
from.** Every 2021 value that is not inherited from the 2004 amendment matches
**СП 54.13330 cl. 5.11 exactly** — the 10 m² two-person bedroom and the 6 m²
kitchen zone included. Laid against both ancestors, AzDTN 2.7-2 cl. 5.7 reads as
the 2004 Azerbaijani text **updated to the Russian successor's schedule**, with
**one** deliberate national departure:

| value | AZ 2004 amdt | СП 54.13330 | **AzDTN 2.7-2, 2021** |
|---|---|---|---|
| living room, 1 otaq | 14 | 14 | **15** ← ① changed, against **both** |
| living room, 2+ | 16 | 16 | 16 |
| bedroom (two persons) | 8 (—) | 8 (10) | 8 (10) |
| kitchen | 8 | 8 | 8 |
| kitchen zone in diner | *type absent* | 6 | 6 |
| kitchen-niche | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| mansard bedroom/kitchen | 7 | 7 | 7 |
| **entry wardrobe, 1-otaq flat** | — | **—** | **2,5** ← ② added, no Russian counterpart |

**This sharpens the transcription note's finding 5.** It read the 15/16 pair as
*"AzDTN's own"* against a Russian line that says 14/16. That is now exact and
better evidenced: across the whole of cl. 5.7 Azerbaijan made **one value change
and one addition**. Every other value in the clause — **including the 6,0 this
note is about** — is a transcription of an older text.

⚠️ **The Russian mansard proviso even carries its condition across intact**:
СП 54.13330 allows 7 m² *«при условии, что общая жилая комната (гостиная) имеет
площадь не менее 16 м²»*, and AzDTN 2.7-2 allows 7 m² *«bu halda ümumi yaşayış
otağının sahəsi 16 m²-dən az olmamalıdır»*. Same rule, same proviso, same
number.

⚠️ **One place cl. 5.7's neighbour does diverge, and it is a real Azerbaijani
choice**: СП 54.13330 cl. 5.12 makes the 2,7 m height **climate-conditional**
(2,5 m outside the listed subzones), while AzDTN 2.7-2 cl. 5.8 imposes 2,7 m
**nationally with no carve-out**. The profile already records this correctly.

⚠️ **And that is itself an argument about the 6,0.** It is not a considered
Azerbaijani judgement about Azerbaijani kitchens; it is a value carried across
from СП 54.13330 along with the room type it belongs to — and carried across
**with the same missing whole-room figure**. The gap was inherited too.

## 7. What Azerbaijan *does* publish that bounds this room

Nothing floors the kitchen-diner. Four things constrain the space around it.

### 7.1 Cədvəl 1 — whole-apartment areas, recommended, state fund only

AzDTN 2.7-2 cl. 5.1:

> «Dövlət və bələdiyyə mənzil fondunun yaşayış binalarında mənzillərin,
> otaqların sayına və sahəsinə görə minimal ölçülərinin cədvəl 1 əsasında
> (balkonların, terrasların, eyvanların, lociyaların, şüşəbəndlərin,
> isidilməyən köməkçi otaqların və mənzilin tamburunun sahələri nəzərə
> alınmamaq şərtilə) qəbul edilməsi **tövsiyə olunur**.»

*"In residential buildings of the **state and municipal** housing fund, it is
**recommended** that the minimum dimensions of apartments, by number of rooms
and area, be adopted on the basis of Table 1 (excluding the areas of balconies,
terraces, verandas, loggias, glazed enclosures, unheated auxiliary rooms and the
apartment vestibule)."*

**Cədvəl 1 — «Mənzillərin tövsiyə edilən ümumi sahəsi, m²»** — recommended total
apartment area by number of habitable rooms, tabulated for *şəhər, qəsəbə*
(city/town) over *kənd* (village).

⚠️ **THE TABLE IS NOT REPRODUCED, and that is a standing posture rather than a
choice made here.** `minima.md` §7.1 established that Cədvəl 1 is substantially
**SNiP 2.08.01-89\* Table 5 carried forward** — same row structure, same column
structure, same exclusion list — and that transcribing its *selection and
arrangement* is precisely the infringement findings §7.6 items 5 and 7 name.
§7.1's words: *"Citing two anchor values individually is safe; transcribing the
table is precisely the thing the posture forbids, and the fact that it would be
transcribing a **twice**-carried table makes it worse, not better."*

Cells cited **individually**, confined to the 1–4 band C13 actually promises, for
*şəhər, qəsəbə*: **1 room 28–38 m²**, **2 rooms 44–53 m²**, **3 rooms 56–65 m²**,
**4 rooms 70–77 m²**. `src: az_azdtn_2_7_2`, `ref: cl. 5.1 Table 1`, `conf:
verified`, `force: recommended`. The village row and the 5- and 6-room columns are
read and **not** carried into this repo. `docs/research/az-market-default-against-practice.md`
§7.1 cites the same four cells and records how the row was identified from a
`pypdf` extraction whose row labels trail their values.

And immediately after, the carve-out that matters most for this engine:

> «**Özəl** mənzil fonduna aid yaşayış binalarındakı mənzillərin sahəsi,
> otaqların sayı və tərkibi **sifarişçi tərəfindən** müəyyən edilir.»

*"The area, number of rooms and composition of apartments in residential
buildings belonging to the **private** housing fund are determined **by the
client**."*

⚠️ **So Cədvəl 1 does not bound a private-sector plan at all**, while cl. 5.7's
per-room minima — phrased *«az olmamalıdır»*, shall not be less than — apply
generally. The norm is deliberately loose on apartment totals and firm on room
minima. Any use of Cədvəl 1 as an engine bound must carry both qualifiers.

### 7.3 Widths and height

AzDTN 2.7-3 cl. 5.1, in **mandatory** phrasing — *«qəbul edilməlidir»* — unlike
the areas above it in the same clause:

> «Otaq və yerləşgələrin eni aşağıda qeyd olunan ölçülərdən az olmayaraq qəbul
> edilməlidir: - yaşayış otaqları - 3,0 m; - mətbəx - 2,6 m; - dəhliz - 1,4 m;

⚠️ **Note the register shift inside a single clause**: cl. 5.1's *areas* are

## 9. Below the norms — Azerbaijani practice

### 9.1 MİDA publishes no space standard

**Mənzil İnşaatı Dövlət Agentliyi**, the state agency that builds subsidised
*güzəştli mənzil* housing, was checked as the most plausible source of a
non-normative Azerbaijani space standard. It publishes none.

- MİDA's own sales list for the Yevlax complex (179 apartments) discloses
  **`Mənzil №` / `Otaq sayı` / `Ümumi sahə` / `Qiymət`** — apartment number,
  room count, total area, price. **No per-room breakdown of any kind.**
- Its contractor and aggregator pages give whole-apartment ranges only
  (Yasamal phase 2: 40,12–84,47 m² across studio/1/2/3-room).
- The *güzəştli mənzil* Əsasnamə governs eligibility and finance, not design.

**No Azerbaijani source — state, developer or listing — was found labelling a
room `mətbəx-yemək otağı` with an area.** Real-estate portals expose room count
and total area only; there is no per-room kitchen-dining field to search.

⚠️ This negative covers **indexed text**. Marketing floor-plan *images* could
carry the label baked into the graphic; that avenue was not exhausted.

### 9.2 No Azerbaijani teaching source states the number either

Fourteen targeted searches against AzMİU, UNEC, Azerbaijani academic
repositories and design literature returned **no textbook, lecture note or
methodological guide** stating a whole-room area for a `mətbəx-yemək otağı`, and
**no rule-of-thumb dining-zone allowance**.

⚠️ Two traps were surfaced and rejected rather than reported as findings.
Azerbaijani sources *do* publish m²-per-person figures for **yeməkxana** — 0,7
m²/person, 10 m² per 50 seats — but these are **public-catering** norms for
canteens and school dining halls, a different building type under a different
discipline. They must not be mistaken for a residential kitchen-diner figure.

## 10. Comparators — none of these has force in Azerbaijan

*Every row is `foreign_not_applicable`. Recorded so the team can see what other
jurisdictions chose, never to borrow their authority. AzDTN 2.7-2 is a
first-edition national norm that explicitly repealed its Soviet ancestor; it
does not incorporate any of these by reference.*

| jurisdiction | instrument | clause | figure | measured on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Russia** | СП 54.13330.**2022** | 5.11 | **6 m²** | **zone** — no whole-room figure exists |
| **Kazakhstan** | СП РК 3.02-101-2012 | 4.4.4.21 | **6 m² + 6 m²** | **two zones** — cooking **and dining** |
| Kazakhstan *(repealed)* | СНиП РК 3.02-43-2007 | Tab. 1 | 18 m² | whole room, top voluntary comfort class only |
| **Uzbekistan** | ШНК 2.08.01-19 | 3.8 | 8 m² for `кухня` | no kitchen-diner area; the term appears once, for ceiling height |
| **Belarus** | СН 3.02.01-2019 | 4.1 | **none** | no room-area table at all — areas go to the design brief |
| **Ukraine** | ДБН В.2.2-15:2019 + Зміна №1 | 5.19 | 8 m² (7 in a one-room flat) | `кухня` only; `кухня-їдальня` shares that number |
| **Georgia** | — | — | **none** | no residential room-area rule; the Soviet SNiP is **not** on its grandfather list |
| **Turkey** | Planlı Alanlar İmar Yönetmeliği | Art. 29 | 3,30 m² for `mutfak` | whole room; **no combined category** — merged spaces take the **additive sum** |

### 10.1 The two comparators that matter, verbatim

**Russia — СП 54.13330.2022 cl. 5.11**, read first-hand:

> «Площадь жилых комнат и вспомогательных помещений в квартирах должна быть, м²,
> не менее: 14 - общей жилой комнаты в однокомнатной квартире; 16 - общей жилой
> комнаты в квартирах с числом жилых комнат две и более; 8 - спальни (10 - на
> двух человек); 8 - кухни; **6 - кухонной зоны в кухне-столовой.**»

⭐ **This is AzDTN 2.7-2 cl. 5.7, value for value, with one exception.**

| | СП 54.13330.2022 | AzDTN 2.7-2 cl. 5.7 |
|---|---|---|
| living room, 1-room flat | 14 | **15** ← the only divergence |
| living room, 2+ rooms | 16 | 16 |
| bedroom (two persons) | 8 (10) | 8 (10) |
| kitchen | 8 | 8 |
| **kitchen zone in diner** | **6** | **6** |
| kitchen-niche | 5 | 5 |
| mansard bedroom/kitchen | 7 | 7 |

Russia's definitions run parallel too: `кухня` is *«помещение **с обеденной
зоной**…»* — a room **with a dining zone** — exactly as AzDTN's `mətbəx` is a
room for cookery *and the taking of food*. **§3.1's taxonomy argument holds in a
second, independent instrument**, and there too the 8 m² kitchen already
contains eating while the kitchen-diner gets only a zone figure.

**Kazakhstan — СП РК 3.02-101-2012 cl. 4.4.4.21**, read first-hand, and the one
regional instrument that closes the gap AzDTN leaves open:

> «Площадь кухонной зоны в кухне-столовой должна быть не менее 6 м², **площадь
> обеденной зоны – не менее 6 м²**.»

*"The area of the cooking zone in a kitchen-dining room shall be not less than
6 m², **the area of the dining zone — not less than 6 m²**."*

⚠️ **This is the single most decision-relevant comparator in the note, and it
disagrees with §3.2.** Kazakhstan uses the identical two-zone construction and
**does** quantify the dining zone — at 6 m², implying a whole room of **≥ 12 m²**,
half again as much as the ≈8 m² that AzDTN's own taxonomy and the ergonomic
layer both suggest. §13 takes this up.

⚠️ Note the framing before borrowing it: Kazakhstan's baseline kitchen is
**9–12 m²** against Azerbaijan's 8, so its whole schedule is more generous, and
its own preface calls the code *«приемлемые решения… не является единственным
способом»* — acceptable deemed-to-satisfy solutions, not the only route to
compliance.

### 10.2 What the comparator sweep establishes

**The gap is regional, not Azerbaijani.** Of eight jurisdictions, **exactly one**
publishes a live whole-room figure for a combined kitchen-dining room — and
Turkey reaches it by refusing the category altogether and summing the parts.
Russia, Azerbaijan's closest legal relative, has the identical zone-only
structure. Belarus publishes no room areas at all. Kazakhstan quantifies both
zones but still no room.

⚠️ **So the absence found in §2 is not an Azerbaijani drafting oversight that
some other Azerbaijani instrument might repair.** It is how this entire family of
norms handles the type. That materially raises confidence in the negative — and
correspondingly lowers the odds that any unexamined Azerbaijani source is hiding
the number.

## 12. Side findings — checkable, and one is wrong today

### 12.1 ⚠️ `clear_widths_mm` cites the wrong clause

`profiles.AZ.rooms.clear_widths_mm.habitable_room.market_default` and
`.kitchen.market_default` both carry `src: "az_azdtn_2_7_3"`, `ref: "cl. 5.4"`.

**AzDTN 2.7-3 cl. 5.4 reads** *«Evin qrunt əsası və konstruksiyaları AzDTN
2.1-1-in tələblərinə görə…»* — the house's **ground base and structures**. It
contains no widths.

The 3,0 m and 2,6 m widths are in **cl. 5.1**. The section-5 clause list of
AzDTN 2.7-3 is: 5.1 areas/widths/proportion, 5.2 heights, 5.3–5.10 structures.

**The values are correct; the citation is not.** Same class of defect as ticket
69's, in a field no guard reads.

### 12.2 An AzDTN proportion rule that is in no artefact

AzDTN 2.7-3 cl. 5.1 closes:

> «Yaşayış otağının uzunluğunun eninə nisbətən **2 dəfədən çox olmayaraq** qəbul
> edilməsi tövsiyə olunur.»

*"It is **recommended** that a living room's length be adopted as **not more
than 2 times** its width."*

A **2:1 aspect cap** on habitable rooms. It appears nowhere in
`room-constraints.json`. `recommended`, and from the detached-house norm, so it
would degrade the same way the widths do — but it is a shape rule from a
regulator, in an engine whose whole business is shape, and it is currently
invisible. Flagged, not adopted; it belongs to whoever owns
`rectangularisation.md`'s aspect handling.

## 13. Bearing on ticket 70's three options

The research does not choose. It changes what each option costs.

**Option 1 — post no statutory floor.** *Strengthened.* The negative is now
exhaustive rather than assumed, so emptying `az_area` is honest about a gap
that has been searched for and shown to be real. ⚠️ But §3.2 shows the fallback
would be the **ergonomic 4,6**, which is **below** even the zone figure — so
option 1 loosens the room by 1,4 m² against today. The loss is larger than
"discards a floor the law genuinely states" implied.

**Option 2 — derive a whole-room floor.** *Sharpened, given an anchor it did not
have — and given a range rather than a point.* §3.2 supplies three routes:
**8,0** from AzDTN's own taxonomy, **7,9** from zone-plus-ergonomics, **12,0**
from the one regional norm that quantifies the dining zone. ⚠️ **The tidiest
form of this option is dead** — §2.3 shows AzDTN quantifies no dining zone, so
"zone plus the norm's dining requirement" cannot be computed from Azerbaijani
law. ⚠️ **And the spread is the finding, not a failure to converge**: 8 is a
floor argument, 12 is a comfort judgement, and choosing between them is a
product decision this research cannot make for the ticket. Whatever is adopted
is `engine_choice`.

⚠️ **A note on ADR 0027 for whichever number is picked.** The band 8–12 spans a
50 % difference in how often this room type will bind. The temptation is to
read the low end as "safer" because it refuses fewer candidates. **That is the
argument ADR 0027 forbids.** The yield each choice costs is a consequence to
record; the reason to pick 8 rather than 12 has to be that 8 is what the
evidence supports, and §3.2 says it supports it as a *lower bound* — not as the
number an architect would draw.

**Option 3 — give the model a zone.** *Strengthened, and it is the only option
that transcribes rather than derives.* §3 shows the zone is not a drafting
quirk: the norm's definitions are **built on** the zone distinction, and
`mətbəx` vs `mətbəx-yemək otağı` vs `taxça-mətbəx` differ by nothing else. §10.2
adds that **the entire regional family drafts this way** — Russia zone-only,
Kazakhstan two zones explicitly — so a zone concept is not an Azerbaijani
peculiarity the engine would be over-fitting to. An engine with a zone could
post AzDTN's 6,0 on the thing AzDTN wrote it about, and Kazakhstan shows what a
second zone constraint looks like when a regulator does write one. ⚠️ It remains
the deep option ADR 0014 constrains.

⚠️ **One correction to the ticket's framing.** It reads the defect as
under-constraint *"by whatever the dining part takes — a quantity AzDTN leaves
unstated."* §3.2 sharpens this: the room's unzoned equivalent is floored at
**8,0**, so the shortfall is not merely unknown, it is **at least 2,0 m²** on
the norm's own internal logic — the engine floors the zoned room below the
unzoned one. That is an ordering inversion, and it is a stronger statement of
the defect than the ticket currently makes.
