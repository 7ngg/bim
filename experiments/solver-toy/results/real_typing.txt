2. IS IT EDGE TYPING? -- how much of a real boundary a preset can see
==========================================================================
   A preset is a four-vector over BBOX edges. A face off the bbox is
   typed only through the notch it bounds, so the share of true
   boundary lying on a bbox edge bounds what a preset can express.
    cap: on a bbox edge  p10 0.638  median 0.737  p90 0.834   exterior_fraction at corpus_median median 0.786
   real: on a bbox edge  p10 0.445  median 0.667  p90 0.793   exterior_fraction at corpus_median median 0.766
   -> a third of a real boundary is off its own bbox, but the notch
      branch recovers most of the typing: NOT the binding constraint.
